How to play
  1. ⚡ Storm Power grows +1 every turn (the pips). Cards cost power.
  2. 🃏 Each turn, tap one card. The enemy secretly picks one too — then both storms hit at once.
  3. 🔄 Counter their element and their storm fizzles:
    💧 douses 🔥 · 🔥 melts ❄️ · ❄️ stills 💨 · 💨 scatters 💧
  4. 🏠 Cards of your faction's element cost 1 less.
  5. 🌡️ Damage heats the planet. If Doomsday hits 100%, everyone loses.
